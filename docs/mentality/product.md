# Product

What Colonaiz sells and why, as the founder described it on 14–15 November 2026.

## The idea

- We produce periodic reports for a business about its competitors.
- We find the competitors ourselves, all over the internet: in the client's country, their continent and the world, depending on the client's scale.
- For each competitor we track job openings, blogs, social media and the website, and what the people of the company post.
- We also track their pricing and their new products, and look at how the products work. We investigate the company to gain the maximum information about it.
- The output is a PDF of about 15–20 pages, full of reports, charts, facts and data. It tells the client how their competitors are working, how they are growing and where they are investing more.
- With the document, the client knows how to update their own business so competitors never get one step ahead.

## How the PDF looks

Stated by the founder on 20 November 2026, after seeing a first version styled like the website (dark pages, glowing planet, gradient headlines):

- The client PDF must be formal, "like a business suit", and official: the kind of document a client expects to receive, with a normal (white) background.
- The website's dark space look does not carry over to the PDF.

## What we do

We do business insights. Our goal is to be BI as a service for small and mid-size companies.

- We help businesses have all the analysis documents they must have, delivered to them as products.
- This lets startups focus more on their vision and their products.
- We have a technology that monitors a huge database of startups all over the world, and we provide insights based on that monitoring system.
- The database holds 300k startups all over the world, gathered from G2, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo and more than 100 startup listings. The founder says that is more than any other by far (22 November 2026), and wants the website to mention it.
- Beside the listing websites, we also check the VCs, accelerators, angel investors and incubators of the 50 top countries: USA, Canada, Australia, UK, Netherlands, Germany, UAE, Türkiye, China and 40 more (founder, 22 November 2026, for the "Where does your data come from?" answer).

What it gives the client: a better understanding of the market they work in. They need to know which direction to go, whether that is adding a new feature or being conservative about adding features. They save time and money, become more productive and improve their performance. They can lead their company better and make better decisions.

Who it is for: small and mid-size companies. The founder also said (15 November 2026) that it may be good for people who want to build a startup in a specific market and have not launched yet.

Monitoring competitors and producing regular reports is what one product, Star Track, does. It is not the description of the whole company.

## What we monitor

We monitor the client's competitors, their market, and everything that can affect their business.
- We monitor changes in our clients' competitors' websites periodically (prices, products, hiring, websites, social media, and the market around them) and record every tiny movement for the client (founder, 22 November 2026).

## Metrics the regular document can include

The founder's list, 15 November 2026:

- Prices
- Features and products
- Job opportunities
- New promotions or assignments
- Blog updates
- Team size and roles
- Website traffic
- Followers on social media
- Engagement on social pages
- Market trend in their category
- Chart of Google Trends keywords in their market
- Number of unsatisfied users of their competitors
- Number of unsatisfied users of their own company
- Number of mentions of their own company
- Number of mentions of their competitors
- How many fundraises their competitors have publicly announced
- Number of businesses created in their category
- Cities of the businesses created in their category
- Cities of their competitors
- VC, grant and angel investor programs specifically for their category
- Page rank of their competitors on specific keywords, and how the rank has changed since the last report
- Failed programs of their competitors
- Dead projects and businesses, and how this has changed since the last report
- Active threats for the businesses in this category
- Competitors running Google ads on the client's company name
- Competitors' ads in LLM responses in the client's market
- General insights related to the company's specific category (for example, the number of flights for a tourist agency)
- Regulation updates related to their category
- Trends of global platforms (ChatGPT, Meta, TikTok and others)
- Backlinks of the competitors and of the client

## The vision

We are like a planet of businesses, gathered from all over the internet: a world of data and analysis that extracts the maximum from that data and provides the insights a founder or business operator would otherwise need a lot of time to gather themselves.

## Why a business needs it

- Companies and businesses grow and change fast, and metrics and parameters change fast with them.
- A business needs clean data to find its path and keep growing at the pace of the market. It needs to know how the others work and what is going on in the market.
- A team of business analysts is expensive for a small company, and doing the work yourself takes a lot of time.
- We do this intelligence work for them.

## Who does the work

Human business analysts, who review the reports and use cutting-edge technology to do the work. The people lead; the technology is their tool.
