# Mentality

How we think about this business. These files are written for agents (and people) working on Colonaiz, so that their decisions, copy and designs follow our way of thinking rather than generic defaults.

## How to use this folder

- Read it before doing business-facing work: copy, design, product or positioning decisions.
- When what you are about to do conflicts with something written here, follow what is written here, or raise the conflict.

## How it is maintained

- Each file covers one topic and is listed in the index below.
- Whenever the founder shares an idea, a principle or a way of thinking about the business, it is added to the fitting file, or a new file is created and listed here.
- When the thinking changes, the existing entry is revised instead of a contradicting one being added.
- Entries record what was actually said. Nothing here is invented or padded.

## Index

- [Product](product.md): what we sell, the vision, why a business needs it, and who does the work.
- [Messaging](messaging.md): tone, accepted copy, wording rules, and the questions the page must answer.
- [Website](website.md): what the website is for: leading clients to buy and gaining their trust.
