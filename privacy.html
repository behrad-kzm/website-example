The short version

  • This website uses no advertising cookies. It uses Google Analytics cookies only if you accept them, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • We collect your details only when you give them to us: when you book a call, write to us or become a client.

This summary is here to help you. The full text below is what applies.

Who we are

colonAIz is the business that operates the website colonaiz.com and provides Star Track. We decide how the personal data described in this policy is used, which makes us the "controller" of it in the language of data protection law.

For any privacy question or request, write to sales@colonaiz.com.

Visitors of this website

You can read this website without telling us who you are. We do not ask you to create an account, and the website does not place advertising cookies in your browser.

Some technical data is still processed when a page loads, because the internet cannot work without it:

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider receives your IP address, your browser type and the time of your visit, and keeps them for a short period for security and to fix errors.
  • Fonts and code libraries. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts and a graphics library from the jsDelivr network. To deliver these files, those providers receive your IP address and browser details. They do not receive anything you type.
  • Booking a call. The "Book a call" buttons open Calendly, a separate service. What you enter there (your name, your email address, the time you choose and anything you write in the form) is collected by Calendly for us, and Calendly may use its own cookies on its pages. Calendly's privacy policy applies there.
  • Analytics, only with your consent. We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use the website: which pages they open, how they arrived, the type of device and browser, and their approximate location (country or city). When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Until you accept, no analytics cookie is stored, and Google receives only basic signals without cookies that do not identify your browser. If you accept, Google Analytics stores cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) that keep for up to two years. Google provides this service to us as our processor, and we have turned off its use for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. If you decline after accepting, we delete the analytics cookies from your browser.

Clients and people who contact us

When you book a call, write to us or buy Star Track, we handle:

  • Contact details: your name, work email address, phone number if you give it, your company and your role.
  • Information about your business: your website, your product, your market and the competitors you mention. Most of this is company information, not personal data.
  • Our conversations: emails, messages, call notes and feedback.
  • Billing data: the invoice details of your company, what you bought and when you paid. Payments are handled by our payment provider or your bank. We do not see or store your full card number.

We get this data from you directly. If a colleague gave us your details as a contact for your company, we got them from that colleague.

Public information in our reports

Star Track reports are about companies: their prices, products, hiring, websites, traffic, funding and markets. To build them we keep a database of startups, gathered from public listing websites and directories, from the public pages of investors, accelerators and incubators, and from the companies' own websites and public social media pages.

Most of this is not personal data. But company information sometimes includes information about people in their professional role, for example:

  • the names and job titles of founders, executives and team members that a company or the person published;
  • the authors of public blog posts and announcements;
  • public posts made on behalf of a company, or by its people about the company's business;
  • public job advertisements, which sometimes name a hiring contact.

Here is how we limit this:

  • We collect from public sources only. We do not access private accounts, private messages, or pages behind a login that we are not allowed to use.
  • We look at people only in their business role. We do not build profiles of private life, and we do not collect sensitive data such as health, religion, political opinions or family details.
  • We do not collect contact details to sell them, and we do not sell lists of people.
  • Reports go to the client who ordered them, under terms that forbid resale and publication.

If you are named in one of our sources and you want to know what we hold about you, or you want it corrected or removed, write to us. See Your rights.

If you represent a company and you believe our database holds wrong information about it, we are also happy to correct it.

Automated tools and AI

We use crawlers and AI systems to gather and organise the public information behind our reports, and a specialist from our team checks every report before it is delivered.

We do not make decisions about individual people by automated means, and nothing we do produces a legal effect on a person. When we use an AI provider to process information, it works for us under a contract, and we do not allow our clients' confidential information to be used to train that provider's general models.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who work for us, such as hosting, email, scheduling (Calendly), website analytics (Google Analytics, only with your consent), payments, accounting, and cloud and AI processing. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Our clients, who receive reports that can contain the public professional information described in Public information in our reports.
  • Professional advisers, such as our lawyers and accountants, when they need it.
  • Authorities, when a law or a valid legal order requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business or a part of it is sold or merged. The data stays protected by this policy.

Transfers to other countries

We work with clients and service providers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live. When we transfer personal data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland to a country that their law does not consider adequate, we use the protections the law provides, such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and booked calls: while we are in contact, and for a reasonable period after our last conversation in case you come back to us.
  • Client data and reports: for as long as you are a client, and afterwards for as long as we need them to answer questions about our work and to defend legal claims.
  • Invoices and payment records: for the period tax and accounting law requires.
  • Public information in our database: while it stays relevant to describing a company and its market. We update it as the sources change, and we delete personal data from it when a valid request asks us to.
  • Server logs: for a short period, set by our hosting provider.
  • Website analytics: cookies keep for up to two years in your browser, and Google Analytics keeps the data for the retention period we set in it, at most 14 months.

When we no longer need data, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Security

We protect the data we hold with measures that fit its sensitivity: encrypted connections, limited access for the people who need it, and providers with a good security record. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach happens that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights over your personal data:

  • to ask whether we hold data about you and to receive a copy of it;
  • to have wrong or incomplete data corrected;
  • to have your data deleted;
  • to object to our use of your data, including any use based on legitimate interest and any direct marketing;
  • to ask us to restrict the use of your data while a question is being solved;
  • to receive the data you gave us in a portable format;
  • to withdraw a consent you gave, at any time.

To use a right, write to the address in Contact. It costs nothing. We may ask for information to confirm who you are, and we answer within one month. We will not treat you differently because you used a right.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can also complain to the data protection authority of your country. We would like the chance to solve the problem with you first.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law: we do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can ask to know, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you in the same way.

Children

Our website and our service are for businesses. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will tell our clients by email.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: sales@colonaiz.com.
