What Star Track is

Star Track is a business intelligence service. We find the competitors of your business, monitor them and the market around them, and deliver our findings to you as a report (a PDF document).

A report can cover subjects such as pricing and plans, products and features, hiring and team size, website and blog changes, website traffic, social media activity, funding news, and trends in your market. The exact contents depend on your business, your market and the information that is publicly available about your competitors. Two reports for two different clients will not contain the same sections.

We build the reports with our own database, crawlers and software, including AI systems, and a specialist from our team checks every report before it is sent to you.

Limits of coverage

We monitor your competitors at regular intervals and we work to notice every meaningful movement. But we do not commit to cover all pivots and all changes. Some of them may be missed. For example:

  • a website that blocks automated access or shows different content to different visitors or countries;
  • a competitor that is new, very small or working silently and has not appeared in any source yet.

We choose the competitors in your report with our own method and judgment. You can tell us about a company you want included or removed and we will consider it, but we do not promise that the list contains every competitor you have.
